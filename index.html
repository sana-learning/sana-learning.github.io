ABOUT SANA

Independent. Practical. International.

SANA Learning & Publishing is an independent digital learning and professional services project founded in Belgrade, Serbia.

Founded by author Nikola Aralica, SANA combines structured learning, professional writing and independent publishing support in one accessible digital environment.

Our approach is simple: useful knowledge should lead to practical action.

Based in Europe. Working worldwide.
